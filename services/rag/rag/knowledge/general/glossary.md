# Glossary

## Notional
The amount of money the client puts into the product.

## Tenor
How long the product lasts, from start to maturity. Entered in calendar days.

## Underlying
The market price or rate the product's payoff depends on, for example the Nifty 50 index or the USD/INR exchange rate.

## Coupon
The fixed return the product promises, quoted per year. It is the most the client can earn from an ELN or a DCD.

## Strike
The reference level the final price is compared with. For an ELN it is usually the starting level. For a DCD it is the exchange rate at which the deposit may be converted.

## Barrier and knock-in
A barrier is a lower price level, for example 80% of the starting level. If the underlying falls below it, the product is knocked in, which means the client starts to bear the fall in the underlying. Before that, only the coupon is received.

## European and American barrier
A European barrier is checked only on the final day. An American barrier is checked on every daily close, so it is easier to breach.

## Protection level
For a capital-protected note, the share of the principal the client gets back at maturity, whatever the market does (for example 100%). It depends on the issuer staying solvent and holds only at maturity.

## Participation rate
For a capital-protected note, the share of any rise in the underlying that the client receives (for example 60%).

## Cap
An upper limit on the extra return. Above it, further gains in the underlying are not paid out.

## Alternate currency
In a dual currency deposit, the second currency the deposit may be repaid in if the exchange rate crosses the strike.

## Low, base and high case
In forecast mode, thousands of possible price paths are simulated. The low, base and high cases are the paths ending near the 5th, 50th (median) and 95th percentile of final values. They are scenarios, not predictions.

## Probability of loss
The share of simulated paths in which the client ends up with less than the amount invested.

## Probability of knock-in
For an ELN with a barrier, the share of simulated paths in which the barrier is breached.
